Loot distributor

Option A Option B

Please choose one of the two distribution methods

Option A:

This method will distribute the coins evenly among players without converting the excess into a lower value piece.

Option B:

This option will distribute the coins evenly among players and convert the excess into a lower value piece.

Option A:

This method will distribute the coins evenly among players without converting the excess into a lower value piece.

For example, if the party has 3 members and the following loot is obtained: 20 gold pieces, 47 silver pieces and 75 copper pieces; the loot will be distributed as follows:
Each player will receive:

  • 6 gold pieces
  • 15 silver pieces
  • 25 copper pieces
There are 2 gold pieces, 2 silver pieces and 0 copper pieces left over.
